I am a first-year Ph.D. student at Zhejiang University, working with Prof. Guofeng Zhang at Zhejiang University and Prof. Hao Zhao at Tsinghua University. I study world models that connect predictive representations, latent dynamics, and efficient embodied control.

Previously, I completed my undergraduate study at Chu Kochen Honors College, Zhejiang University.

01Research direction

From representation
to action.

A useful world model should learn both what an action does and which action realizes an intent.

01 / Representation

What makes a latent space useful?

Predictive representations that remain stable, informative, and grounded in physical change.

02 / Intent

How does an intent become an action?

Connecting goal-conditioned latent change to deployable control without losing representation quality.

03 / Control

Can embodied learning be more efficient?

Compact world models, flexible time scales, and responsive control with less online search.
