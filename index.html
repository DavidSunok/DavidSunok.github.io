Junhan Sun 孙俊涵

I am a first-year Ph.D. student at Zhejiang University, working with Prof. Guofeng Zhang at Zhejiang University and Prof. Hao Zhao at Tsinghua University.

My research connects predictive representations, world models, and embodied control. I am interested in how learned models turn visual goals into efficient, purposeful action.

Previously: Chu Kochen Honors College, Zhejiang University.
